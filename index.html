About Green Campus

Green Campus is an eco-awareness initiative designed to encourage students to protect the environment and adopt sustainable lifestyles. Our mission is to reduce pollution, save natural resources, encourage recycling and create a healthy environment for future generations.

Eco-Friendly Practices

Small steps can make a big difference!

♻️

Waste Management

Separate waste, recycle materials and reduce plastic usage.

💧

Water Conservation

Save water by closing taps and avoiding unnecessary wastage.

⚡

Energy Saving

Switch off lights and fans when they are not needed.

🌳

Tree Plantation

Plant trees and protect greenery around the campus.

🚲

Green Transport

Use bicycles, walking and public transport to reduce pollution.

☀️

Solar Energy

Encourage renewable energy for a cleaner environment.

Smart Eco Tips

  • Carry reusable water bottles.
  • Avoid single-use plastic.
  • Use both sides of paper.
  • Keep classrooms clean.
  • Switch off electrical appliances.
  • Participate in tree plantation activities.
  • Spread environmental awareness.
